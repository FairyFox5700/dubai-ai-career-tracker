RELOCATION PLAYBOOK

Sponsorship Strategy

Practical steps, hard questions, and offer safeguards for a Dubai move.

Re-verify government rules before acting
01

UAE employment visa workflow

  1. Secure a signed offer from a UAE employer; do not start work on a tourist or visit visa.
  2. The employer applies for the MOHRE work permit or the relevant free-zone authorization.
  3. Complete entry-status change if needed, medical fitness screening, Emirates ID biometrics, health insurance, and residence formalities.
  4. Verify that the contract matches the signed offer before final approval.

Primary guidance: UAE Government · MOHRE overseas work permit

02

Target-employer tiers

Tier 1: multinational employers with established UAE mobility teams—Microsoft, Google Cloud, AWS, Salesforce, NVIDIA, Oracle, IBM, ServiceNow, Databricks.

Tier 2: well-funded AI and technology leaders—G42/Core42/Presight, Careem, Palantir, Celonis.

Tier 3: consulting firms with UAE delivery demand. Confirm the employing entity, permit route, bench policy, and client-site expectations.

03

Position the CV

  • Lead with “AI Engineer who ships enterprise agents from architecture to production.”
  • Quantify customer outcomes, adoption, quality, latency, cost, and evaluation gains.
  • Cluster Azure AI Foundry, Copilot Studio, RAG, Azure AI Search, and evaluation into one deployment story.
  • Show C#/.NET, Python, TypeScript, Kubernetes, Terraform, and distributed-systems depth—not only demos.
  • Add “Open to Dubai relocation; employer-sponsored UAE work authorization required.”
04

Recruiter questions

  • Will the UAE employing entity sponsor the work permit and residence visa for this role?
  • Is relocation support separate from immigration processing?
  • Are any costs recoverable if I leave during probation?
  • Which office, free zone, and legal entity appear on the contract?
  • Is Arabic required in practice, and how much customer-site travel is expected?
05

Red flags

  • Visit-visa work: do not agree to perform productive work before valid authorization.
  • Candidate-paid visa costs: reject requests to pay recruitment, permit, or residency processing fees to an employer or agent.
  • Personal bank accounts, crypto transfers, WhatsApp-only interviews, pressure to resign before a verified contract, or mismatched offer and MOHRE terms.

UAE Government: preparing to work

06

Application cadence

Weekly: 5 high-fit applications, 5 targeted referrals, and 2 recruiter conversations. Apply within 72 hours when possible. Spend 80% of effort on Tier 1/2 employers and 20% on carefully screened consulting roles.

Track response, referral, interview, and offer conversion every Friday; change messaging before increasing volume.

07

Networking

  • Ask Microsoft alumni and Azure customers now based in the UAE for role calibration—not generic referrals.
  • Contact Dubai AI, cloud, platform, and professional-services leaders with one relevant delivery story.
  • Use GITEX, AI Everything, Microsoft/AWS/Google user groups, and technical meetups to build repeated contact.
  • Request a referral only after validating team, level, location, and sponsorship route.
08

Offer due diligence

  • Legal employing entity and work location
  • Base salary and any allowances in AED
  • Bonus target, equity, sign-on, relocation
  • Health insurance coverage and dependants
  • Probation, notice, non-compete, clawbacks
  • Annual leave, flights, remote-work policy
  • Permit/visa owner, timeline, family support
  • Offer-to-contract consistency and start gate

Do not compare packages using an invented EUR conversion. Record only explicit offer values and use a current exchange rate at decision time.
